Common questions
What is the conversion fee?

A 2.5% conversion fee covers BTC→USDT conversion and BTC price movement. Every amount on your dashboard is already after fees.

What does “Luna 27%” mean?

The percent next to a link name is the extra fee you set on that link when you created it. The buyer pays it on top of the price and it is yours: your dashboard keeps showing the base amount with the tag (e.g. $100 · Luna 27%), and the extra fee is added when you cash out, minus the conversion and withdraw fees. It is locked and can't be changed later.

Which amount do I see?

Payment and invoice cards and Telegram alerts show the link's base amount, without the extra fee and the conversion fee (a $100 Luna 27% payment shows $100).

What is a Lightning Address?
It looks like an email and never expires, so Lightning payouts can be sent without pasting a fresh invoice each time. Wallets that give you one: Wallet of Satoshi, Alby, Phoenix, Zeus, Coinos, Primal, Bitkit. Open your wallet, find "Lightning Address" or "Receive → Address", copy it and paste it here.
