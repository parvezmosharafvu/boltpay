FAQ

Common questions

Still stuck? Contact support below.

How do customers pay?

They open your link, choose an amount and pay the Lightning invoice, for example with the "Open in Cash App" button or any Lightning wallet.

When can I sign in?

After an administrator activates your account.

When is my balance settled?

Every day at 7 pm (Dhaka time) a daily statement is created in your dashboard.

How do I withdraw?

Request a withdrawal from your dashboard using one of the methods your administrator has enabled.

Is Boltpay available in Bangla?

Yes. Use the language button at the top to switch between English and Bangla.

How do I get help?

Use the support contacts at the bottom of this page.
